## Where OKX costs you

**The US entity is expensive.** Spot at 0.2000% maker and 0.3500% taker is roughly 2.5 times the global maker rate and 3.5 times the global taker rate. Global futures pricing does not apply to a US account, so any review quoting you 0.02% / 0.05% while you sit in the US is describing a product you can't buy.

**Product menus split hard by region.** EU retail users don't get the high-leverage derivatives available globally. Tokenized stocks, launched in 2026 with roughly 40 underlyings and withdrawable on supported chains, are unavailable in both the US and the EU, and they confer no ownership or voting rights in the underlying company. US users get Event Contracts and the app-only Outcomes product instead of the global book, and Outcomes eligibility excludes at least Florida and Rhode Island.

**There's a criminal settlement in the recent history.** In February 2025 the affiliate named in the settlement, Aux Cayes FinTech Co. Ltd., pleaded guilty in the US to operating an unlicensed money-transmitting business after serving US customers it should have blocked, and agreed to pay more than $504 million: an $84 million penalty plus about $421 million in forfeiture. OKX has since relaunched a US platform built around US registration. The court documents don't establish that the affiliate contracted with every OKX account holder, and OKX's own statements about later monitoring contradict each other, so claims about a government-appointed monitor and its duration can't be confirmed either way.

**There is no deposit insurance.** Not for crypto, not for fiat. Separate insurance funds exist for margin, futures, options and perpetual contracts, which protects the derivatives book, not the spot balance sitting in your account.

**Support has no published clock.** Channels are a chatbot and email, with no phone line and no stated guarantee that a withdrawal review closes within 24 hours. Extra review triggers include large transfers, mismatched ID documents and changes to security settings. G2 reviews include a detailed account of funds frozen for close to a year with KYC documents, stamped bank statements included, rejected repeatedly without explanation. Those are undated user reports rather than verified cases, but they line up with the absence of any published resolution timeline.

**The app assumes you already know what you're doing.** Lite and Pro modes help, and a free demo account exists, but the number of menus is the single most common complaint from newer users. The self-custody wallet carries a harder edge: lose the seed phrase and nobody at OKX can restore access.

## FAQ

**Is OKX safe, and has it been hacked?**
There's no public record of a major hack of the main exchange, and customer funds are mostly held in offline multi-signature wallets. Its predecessor OKEx did suspend withdrawals in October 2020 while a key holder was unavailable to authorize them; that was an operational halt, not a breach. The compliance record is separate: the February 2025 US plea and the $504 million total are part of the same company's history.

**Is OKX available in the US, and what's different?**
Yes, through OKX Inc., formerly OKCoin USA Inc., registered with FinCEN under NMLS #1767779. Fees are 0.2000% / 0.3500% on regular spot, global futures pricing doesn't apply, and the US menu runs Event Contracts and Outcomes rather than the global derivatives book.

**What are OKX's fees?**

**Does OKX have deposit insurance?**
No. Crypto and fiat balances aren't insured. Insurance funds cover margin, futures, options and perpetual contracts only.

Yes. OKX Europe Limited has held a MiCA CASP authorisation from Malta's Financial Services Authority since January 2025, which lets it serve the European Economic Area. That's a Maltese licence, and it comes with product limits for retail users.
